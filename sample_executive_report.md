# 2025-2026 Business Performance Executive Report

## Executive summary
Analytics Suite revenue increased from 84,200 in January 2025 to 186,500 in December 2026. The strongest recurring region is West, while East remains the lowest-performing region. The December 2026 result is a material outlier and should be validated before it is used in a forecast.

## Regional performance
- North grew steadily from 84,200 to 101,400 during the first six months of 2025.
- South generated between 76,800 and 89,200 during July-November 2025 before reaching 186,500 in December 2025 in the source sales table.
- East generated between 69,400 and 84,600 during January-June 2026.
- West generated between 102,300 and 120,700 during July-November 2026 and reached 186,500 in December 2026.

## Cost and efficiency
Operating costs generally increased alongside revenue. The December 2026 cost of 88,400 is substantially above the normal West-region range and should be reviewed with the revenue spike.

## Management questions
1. Was the December increase caused by a large contract, a pricing change, or a data entry issue?
2. Can the West-region operating model be replicated in East and South?
3. Did customer acquisition cost increase during the high-revenue period?

## Recommended decisions
- Validate the December transaction records.
- Separate one-time revenue from recurring revenue.
- Compare margin by region before reallocating sales investment.
- Set a monthly anomaly review for revenue values above 150,000.
