# Analytics Suite Growth Strategy Brief

## Objective
Grow recurring Analytics Suite revenue while protecting operating margin.

## Opportunity view
West provides the strongest current benchmark for sales execution. North demonstrates a stable growth pattern. East should be treated as a focused expansion market because it has consistent month-over-month improvement and lower absolute revenue.

## Strategic options
### Option A: Replicate the West playbook
Document West's acquisition channels, customer profile, sales cycle, and renewal motion. Pilot the playbook with the East sales team.

### Option B: Protect margin first
Investigate operating-cost growth before increasing acquisition spend. Use contribution margin, not revenue alone, as the investment decision metric.

### Option C: Build an enterprise renewal program
If the December spike is confirmed as a renewal event, create a dedicated enterprise renewal forecast and report recurring versus one-time revenue separately.

## Decision criteria
- Recurring revenue contribution
- Operating margin by region
- Customer retention and expansion
- Sales cycle length
- Revenue concentration in top customers

## Recommended next step
Complete the December data reconciliation, then compare the three options using regional margin and recurring revenue evidence. Do not treat the December spike as a baseline until Finance confirms the underlying transactions.
